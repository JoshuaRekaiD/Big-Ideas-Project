Bishops Big Ideas

A school project that became a real mission.

We're a group of grade 9 students at Bishops Diocesan College in Cape Town. What started as a philanthropy assignment turned into something we genuinely care about: fighting food waste in our communities.

How It Started

Our Process to the Problem

Birp is a Cape Town food-rescue initiative created by three Grade 9 students at Bishops Diocesan College: Joshua Fried, Joshua Dembaremba and Bonnar Richards. We started Birp because perfectly good food is often left unsold at the end of the day, while many people in Cape Town struggle with putting food on the table — so we wanted to reduce food waste while raising money to help fight hunger.

We were tasked to explore one specific problem space in our outreach class, Big Ideas. The process to finalising each group's project had three pillars: See, Think, Wonder. All three of us were passionate about tackling both hunger and food waste. We identified the obvious potential bridge between both of these issues and developed Birp.

"Big Ideas come from Small observations."
— The Big Ideas Team

Our Mission & Values
