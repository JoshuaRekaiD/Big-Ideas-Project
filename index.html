Our Promise

100% of Birp's proceeds go to charity.

Birp isn't here to make a profit. Every cent we earn goes directly to organisations fighting food insecurity in South Africa. When you rescue a meal with Birp, you're feeding someone who needs it too.

0 profit kept by us

Why Birp

Good for you. Good for everyone.

Good for you

Great food at up to 70% off — from local businesses you already love.

Good for your wallet

Save hundreds of Rand every month without compromising on quality.

Good for the planet

Every bag you rescue is one less meal going to waste — and a donation to charity.

As a Birp customer you get…

  • Surprise bags at up to 70% off retail
  • Great food from local stores you already know
  • The good feeling of reducing food waste
  • Knowing your money feeds someone in need
  • A quick, app-based way to order in under a minute

As a participating store you get…

  • A new way to recover costs on surplus stock
  • Zero waste and zero guilt on unsold food
  • Exposure to new local customers
  • A simple setup — we handle the platform
  • A visible role in your community's food-security effort
