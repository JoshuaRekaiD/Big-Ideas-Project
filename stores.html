Partner With Us

Turn your surplus into impact.

Join Birp and sell your end-of-day surplus food to local customers at a discount — reducing waste, recovering costs, and doing good in your community. It's free to join.

Why Join

What you get as a Birp partner

Recover lost revenue

Food you'd otherwise throw away becomes income. Every bag sold is money back from stock that would have cost you to discard.

Zero waste, zero guilt

Stop throwing away perfectly good food at the end of every day. Your surplus goes to someone who wants it instead of a landfill.

New customers

Birp users discover your store through the app. Many become regular customers after their first surprise bag experience.

Simple setup

We handle the platform, the payments, and the customer communications. You just prepare the bag and confirm collections.

Community standing

Being a Birp partner signals to your community that you care about sustainability and food security — not just the bottom line.

No upfront cost

Joining Birp is completely free. There are no setup fees, no monthly fees, and no obligation. You list when you have surplus — that's it.

The Product

What is a Birp surprise bag?

A surprise bag is a selection of your end-of-day surplus food, packaged and listed at a discounted price. Customers know the general type of food they're getting, but not the exact contents — that's part of the experience.

  • You set the price and collection window
  • You decide how many bags to list each day
  • Contents vary — that's expected and clearly communicated to customers
  • You provide basic allergen information for the bag type
  • You remain responsible for the food you supply

Responsibilities

What we expect from partners

Birp is built on trust — between stores, customers, and our community. Here's what being a good partner means.

Food safety

All food listed on Birp must comply with South African food safety regulations. Stores are responsible for the preparation, storage, and packaging of food they supply. Birp does not handle food directly.

Accurate allergen information

You must provide honest allergen information for your bag type. Customers rely on this to make safe choices. If contents change significantly, update the listing.

Reliable collection windows

Customers plan their day around your pickup time. Stick to the window you set, or notify us in advance if you need to change it. Consistency builds customer trust.

Honest bag value

A surprise bag should represent genuine value — the contents should be worth meaningfully more than the Birp price. Don't pad bags with items you'd discard anyway.
